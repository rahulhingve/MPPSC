🧠 Key Concept Cards

Drainage system correction

  • Teacher first “Southern drainage” bolte hain, then correct करते हैं: better term = Southern River System / दक्षिणी नदी तंत्र.
  • Reason: ये rivers south direction में बहती हैं.
  • Drainage-wise ये Godavari drainage system ka part माना गया.
  • Godavari drainage system ultimately Bay of Bengal / बंगाल की खाड़ी की ओर जाता है.

Main flow chart — big picture

  • Wardha originates from Satpura/Mahadev range near Vardhan peak, Betul/Multai side.
  • Pench originates around Junnardeo, Chhindwara.
  • Kanhan originates from Chhindwara region.
  • Wainganga originates from Paraswada plateau / Rajo Tal or Rajo village / Mundara, Seoni.
  • Pench joins Kanhan near Nagpur.
  • Kanhan joins Wainganga in Maharashtra side.
  • Wardha + Wainganga मिलकर Pranhita बनाते हैं; Pranhita आगे Godavari में जाती है.

Wainganga side tributaries shown in overview

  • Bawanthadi river joins Wainganga.
  • Dev river joins Wainganga; Dev river par Maldhar waterfall mentioned.
  • Chandan river joins Wainganga; Chandan par Nalseesa Dam mentioned.
  • Wainganga Canal, Dhuti Dam and Gangulpara waterfall were also shown with Wainganga.

🔗 Link Story — ऐसे याद करो
