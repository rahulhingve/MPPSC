🌊 Drainage System vs River — अपवाह तंत्र और नदी

Teacher ne pehle definitions di, kyunki class Pre + Mains level ki hai. Narmada example se samjhaya: Narmada alone = river; Narmada + 41 tributaries = Narmada drainage system.

Drainage System / अपवाह तंत्र

  • Jab koi river apne source/origin point se independently flow kare.
  • Ek definite direction me apni tributaries / सहायक नदियों ke saath chale.
  • Apne confluence point par sea/ocean/lake me mile.
  • Us river + tributaries se jo total network/area banta hai, wahi drainage system/drainage area hai.
  • Example: Narmada + 41 tributaries = Narmada Drainage System.

River / नदी

  • River = Earth surface par flow karne wali water stream / जलधारा.
  • Origin mountain, plateau ya glacier se ho sakta hai.
  • MP me glacier/himnad nahi hai — rivers mostly पर्वत/पठार se originate hoti hain.
  • River kisi other river, sea ya lake se mil sakti hai.
  • Example: Betwa river hai, but independent drainage system nahi, kyunki Betwa Yamuna me milti hai.

Key Difference

  • Drainage system ke liye river ka independent confluence required hai.
  • River kisi doosri river se mil jaye to woh khud ka independent drainage system nahi banati.
  • Narmada river independent hai; Betwa Yamuna me milti hai.
  • 🔥 Exam phrasing: River aur drainage system ko confuse mat karna.

🧭 Madhya Pradesh ke 6 Drainage Systems

MP ke drainage systems ko teacher ne 2 directions me samjhaya: Bay of Bengal side aur Arabian Sea side. Direct exam answer: 6 systems.

Bay of Bengal side / बंगाल की खाड़ी की ओर

  • 1) Ganga–Yamuna Drainage System — MP ka सबसे बड़ा अपवाह तंत्र.
  • 2) Godavari Drainage System — MP ki दक्षिण की नदियां jaise Wardha, Wainganga, Kanhan etc. ultimately Godavari system me jaati hain.
  • 3) Mahanadi Drainage System — MP ka सबसे छोटा अपवाह तंत्र.

Arabian Sea side / अरब सागर की ओर

  • 4) Narmada Drainage System — MP me second largest drainage system, but Narmada river MP ki largest river hai.
  • 5) Tapti Drainage System — Tapti independent flow karti hai.
  • 6) Mahi Drainage System — Mahi bhi independent flow karke Arabian Sea me milti hai.

🔥 Exam Hot

  • MP ke drainage systems = 6. Agar kahin 5 likha ho, teacher ke according 6 mark karna.
  • Largest river ≠ largest drainage system.
  • MP ka largest drainage system = Ganga–Yamuna.
  • MP ka smallest drainage system = Mahanadi.
  • Narmada River largest hai, par drainage system second largest hai.

🔗 Link Story — 6 systems याद करो

🏞️ MP Rivers — Nadiyon ka Mayka & Peninsular Rivers

Teacher ne MP ko ‘Nadiyon ka Mayka’ kaha kyunki adhikansh rivers MP ke mountains/plateaus se janm leti hain. MP me approximately 207 recognized small-big rivers batayi gayi.

Recognized rivers

  • MP me मान्यता प्राप्त छोटी-बड़ी लगभग 207 rivers batayi gayi.
  • Teacher ne kaha random local streams ko count nahi karna; government-recognized rivers ke context me baat hai.
  • MP has one of the largest number of rivers in India — class wording: 'भारत की सबसे ज्यादा नदियां'.

Nadiyon ka Mayka

  • Mayka = janm sthal / birth place.
  • MP ki adhikansh rivers yahin ke पर्वत/पठार se originate hoti hain.
  • Examples: Narmada, Tapti, Betwa, Wainganga, Wardha, Chambal, Shipra, Parvati, Sindh etc.
  • Baad me ye kisi aur state/river/sea me mil sakti hain — par janm MP me hota hai.

Peninsular Rivers

  • MP rivers are Peninsular Rivers / प्रायद्वीपीय नदियां.
  • South India ka northern part = MP, teacher ke explanation me.
  • Peninsula = teen taraf water se घिरा landmass: west Arabian Sea, south Indian Ocean, east Bay of Bengal.
  • Isliye MP se originate hone wali rivers ko Peninsular rivers context me samjha.
