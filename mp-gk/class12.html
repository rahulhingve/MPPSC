💧 जलप्रपात की Definition / Waterfall Concept

Waterfall ka basic concept: river/nala/rainwater jab high place/mountain se neeche girta hai, to waterfall banta hai. Ye flowing-water erosional landform aur water source bhi hota hai.

🧠 Key Notes

  • जलप्रपात = किसी नदी/नाला/वर्षा जल का ऊँचे पर्वत/ऊँचाई से नीचे गिरना.
  • Place jahan river falls from height is called waterfall.
  • Sources: river water, rain water, mountain stream — teeno context class mein aaye.
  • Flowing water neeche land/rock par erosion karta hai; therefore it is an erosional landform.
  • Waterfall natural feature hai, nature ke through banta hai.

🔥 Exam Hot

  • 🔥 Definition + erosional landform + water source — conceptual MCQ ban sakta hai.

🧠 Key Notes

  • Amarkantak area: Kapildhara, Dugdh Dhara, Durga Dhara, Panchdhara, Shambhu Dhara — mostly 2–4 km interval mein.
  • Kapildhara: Kapil Muni ashram/आराधना स्थल; Meghdoot mention; approx 18 m.
  • Dugdh Dhara: paani doodh jaisa safed dikhta hai.
