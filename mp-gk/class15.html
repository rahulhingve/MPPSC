🌬️ Monsoon Mechanism + Why Rainfall Varies

Heat→Air warms→Air becomes light→Rises→Takes moisture→High pressure to low pressure→Rain

Mechanism exactly follows teacher’s explanation: garam hawa halki hoti hai, upar uthti hai, moisture lekar low pressure side jati hai.

Monsoon word

Teacher ne monsoon ko Arabic word “Mausim / Mosin” se linked bataya.

Teacher analogy: 100 kg → 10 gram

Jab air warm hokar light ho jati hai, to upar uthti hai. Teacher ne bola: agar 100 kg ka aadmi 10 gram ho jaye to wo bhi udne lagega.

Teacher analogy: High pressure → Low pressure

Ghar me father ka high pressure ho to student Delhi/Indore jaane ko bhaagta hai jahan low pressure hai. Nature me bhi hawa high pressure se low pressure ki taraf chalti hai.

Arabian Sea branch

  • Western MP / Rajasthan side zyada heat hoti hai.
  • MP me pehle Arabian Sea branch wala monsoon aa sakta hai.
  • Arabian branch ka area coverage zyada bataya.
  • Par Vindhya range parallel hone se hawa kaafi aage nikal jati hai.

🔥 Amount vs Area distinction

  • Area coverage zyada: Arabian Sea branch.
  • Rainfall amount/intensity zyada: Bay of Bengal branch.
  • Teacher ne bola: iss line ko pakad lo, confusion clear ho jayega.

Bay of Bengal branch

  • Satpura mountain aur Maikal ki अर्धचंद्राकार shape se takrati hai.
  • Eastern/Southern MP me strong rainfall.
  • Balaghat district side me heavy rain.

Rain-shadow point

Nimar / Khargone-Khandwa region me kam rainfall ka reason: Satpura ka वृष्टि छाया क्षेत्र / rain shadow area.

Bundelkhand me kam rain ke 3 reasons

  1. Tree cover kam, isliye thanda low-pressure area kam banta.
  2. Sea / Bay of Bengal se distance.
  3. High mountain barrier nahi jo monsoon ko rok kar barsa de.

🔥 Meeting point of branches

Arabian Sea branch aur Bay of Bengal branch ka मिलन स्थल: विंध्य पहाड़ी क्षेत्र.

🔗 Link Story — Monsoon mechanism

Garmi ne hawa ko halka kiya, hawa upar uthi, samundar se paani ko saath liya, high pressure ghar se nikli, low pressure area me pahunchi, pahaad se takra kar baras gayi.
