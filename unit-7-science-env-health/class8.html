🌿 Biodiversity Meaning + Term Origin

Definition / परिभाषा

  • Environment is made of many ecosystems / पारिस्थितिक तंत्र.
  • Different ecosystems contain different varieties of organisms and communities.
  • This visible variety among organisms and communities is called जैव विविधता / Biodiversity.

Term Origin

  • Raymond F. Dasmann used the term “Biological Diversity” in 1968.
  • Walter G. Rosen later shortened/changed it into “Biodiversity” by removing “logical”.
  • Exam memory: Dasmann = Biological Diversity; Rosen = Biodiversity.

🧬 Types of Biodiversity — Genetic, Species, Community

1) आनुवांशिक विविधता / Genetic Diversity

  • एक ही प्रकार के जीव में genes के आधार पर विविधता.
  • Examples from class: dogs — Labrador, desi dog, Pomeranian, Shepherd, “Vodafone वाला cute dog”.
  • Genes अलग-अलग होंगे तो qualities/varieties अलग दिखेंगी.

2) प्रजाति विविधता / Species Diversity

  • Different species together: human, monkey, dog, cat, tree, bird, butterfly etc.
  • जब अलग-अलग प्रजातियों में विविधता देखें तो species diversity.

3) सामुदायिक विविधता / Community or Ecosystem Diversity

  • Two communities / ecosystems compare करें: e.g., forest ecosystem vs river ecosystem.
  • Different habitats have organisms with different ways of living, breathing, surviving.
  • Basis: genetic diversity = genes; species diversity = species; community diversity = ecosystem/community.

Teacher’s Examples

  • Fish varieties: gold fish, desi fish, rohu/food fish etc.
  • Same species varieties → genetic diversity; different species → species diversity; different ecosystems → community diversity.

Equator Rule / भूमध्य रेखा नियम

🛡️ Biodiversity Conservation — In-situ vs Ex-situ

Why Protect?

  • Biodiversity conservation is needed so food chains remain orderly.
  • Each organism should continue getting food; then ecosystem and environment remain secure.
  • Environment, ecosystem and biodiversity are linked.

In-situ / स्व-स्थानी संरक्षण

  • “जो जहाँ पर है, वहीं सुरक्षा दे दो.”
  • Example: Bandhavgarh National Park in Umaria district.
  • Forest and organisms remain in their natural place; government/people protect that area.
  • Teacher jokingly says the most dangerous outside animal is human — two hands, two legs, two eyes, one nose, two ears — because humans exploit everything for survival.

Ex-situ / पर-स्थानी / बाह्य-स्थानी संरक्षण

  • If a species is unsafe in its natural habitat, shift it to another place.
  • Provide food, survival conditions and breeding support.
  • Examples: zoo, botanical garden, seed bank, gene bank, cryopreservation/low-temperature conservation.
  • Teacher critiques zoos: a zoo is like a bigger cage; animals may lose natural hunting qualities and freedom.

Seed Bank + Lahari Bai Example

  • Seed bank preserves important crop species and improved seeds using chemical coating or controlled temperature.
  • Lahari Bai of Dindori, Madhya Pradesh is mentioned as a millet/श्री-अन्न seed conservator.
  • She collects different millet seeds, gives them to farmers in season, and again keeps some quantity so varieties continue.
  • Class claim: she has preserved more than 100 varieties of coarse grains/millets.

Gene Bank + Cryopreservation

  • Gene bank stores genetic material of plants/animals: genes, chromosomes etc.
  • Useful traits of a species can be protected for future breeding/research.
  • Cryopreservation / अति-न्यून ताप संरक्षण stores seeds/genes at very low temperature, often linked with seed banks and gene banks.
  • Fridge/cold-storage analogy: low temperature slows or stops chemical reactions and decay.
  • Premature baby analogy: a baby born before full development is given womb-like conditions for survival — teacher uses this as a conservation-like example.

Botanical Garden + Zoo

  • Botanical gardens conserve different plant varieties, allow research, productivity improvement and better adaptation.
  • Zoo conserves animals by shifting them into managed spaces, but teacher notes it may reduce natural behavior.

🏞️ Protected Area Details + Ex-situ Causes

In-situ Examples to Remember

  • राष्ट्रीय उद्यान / National Park
  • वन्यजीव अभयारण्य / Wildlife Sanctuary
  • जैव मंडल आरक्षित क्षेत्र / Biosphere Reserve
  • पवित्र उपवन / Sacred Grove
  • Formula: जो जहाँ है, वहीं सुरक्षा.

National Park vs Sanctuary vs Biosphere Reserve

  • National Park: government control is strict/complete.
  • Sanctuary: some private activities may get permission.
  • Biosphere Reserve: government controlled; has core zone and buffer zone.
  • Core zone: strict; buffer zone: limited permission possible.

Why Ex-situ is Needed

  • Habitat loss / अधिवास की क्षति.
  • Disease / बीमारी.
  • Over-exploitation / अतिशय शोषण.
  • Genetic reasons / आनुवांशिक कारण.
  • Environmental reasons or disaster / पर्यावरणीय कारण या आपदा.

Seed Bank Analogy with Blood Bank

  • Blood bank stores O+, A+, B+, A−, B−, AB+, AB− etc. so blood is available during accident/emergency.
  • Similarly, seed bank stores important seeds/varieties so they can be used when needed and do not disappear.

Gene Pool / Gene Bank

  • Different plant and animal species’ genetic material is collected and protected.
  • Chromosomes are genetic material made of genes; preserving genes helps preserve future varieties.

Cryopreservation + Botanical Garden + Zoo Photos

  • Cryopreservation uses very low temperature, often liquid nitrogen or cold storage, to keep samples safe for a long time.
  • Botanical gardens grow and research plant varieties; productivity and adaptation can be improved.
  • Zoo contains animals like giraffe, zebra, lion etc.; teacher again jokes about human/child as zoo member.

💧 Wetlands / Ramsar Sites

What is Wetland / आद्र भूमि?

  • Wetlands are areas with both shallow water and land — दलदली क्षेत्र.
  • They provide good conditions for organism growth because moisture and warmth are available.
  • Called आद्र भूमि / Wetland.

Ramsar Convention

  • Meeting took place in Ramsar city, Iran.
  • Year taught: 1971.
  • Internationally important wetlands are designated as Ramsar sites.
  • Goal: protect wetlands because they support high biodiversity; the actual aim is conserving biodiversity, not just the land.

Wetland Classification

  • Coastal wetlands / समुद्र तटीय आद्र भूमि: estuary/ज्वारनदमुख, coral reef/प्रवाल भित्ति, lagoon/लगून, bay/खाड़ी, strait/जलडमरूमध्य, coast/तट, mangrove.
