Q: "Agar mujhe MERIT mein aana hai, to main EXACTLY kya karun?"

MPPSC is structurally predictable. Rule #1: Own MP GK completely. It constitutes 32-38% of the paper.

Rule #2: The "Holy Trinity" strategy. MP GK + ICT (Unit 9) + Current Affairs/Sports. Combined, these yield ~55-60 marks. Master these, and you only need ~25 marks from the massive traditional subjects (History, Geo, Polity, Economy) to cross the safe score of 80+.

Q: "Kya DEFINITELY padhu aur kya SKIP kar sakta hu?"

  • ✅ DEFINITELY (High ROI): MP Tribes (New Unit 10), MP Geography & Economy, ICT & E-Governance, Modern Indian History (Social reforms, 1857), Polity (Articles, Amendments, Commissions).
  • ⚠️ SKIP / SKIM (Low ROI): Deep Ancient/Medieval World History, highly complex physical geography, core theoretical economics, obscure pure science formulas.

⚖️ Polity & Constitution

Avg 9 questions/year. Focus shifted from static to applied.

Top Topics: Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Parliament vs State Leg, Amendments (42, 44, 73, 74).
Trend: Constitutional/Statutory bodies (Election Comm, CAG, SC/ST Comm) are guaranteed hits.
2027 Trap: Tricky assertion-reason questions on Supreme Court judgments vs Parliament acts.

📜 History of India

Avg 10 questions/year. Modern > Ancient > Medieval.

Top Topics: Indus Valley sites, Buddhism/Jainism, Socio-religious reforms, Gandhian Era dates.
Trend: Moving away from Delhi Sultanate battles towards cultural/administrative terms (e.g., Mansabdari, land revenue systems).

🗺️ Geography

Avg 11 questions/year. India Geo dominates World Geo.

Top Topics: Indian physiography (Himalayas, passes), Monsoons, Census 2011 (still relevant!), World grasslands/minerals.
Trend: Map-based matching questions are increasing. Less focus on complex geomorphology.

🔬 Science, Tech & Env

Avg 12 questions/year. Applied > Pure Science.

Top Topics: Human diseases/vitamins, Space missions (ISRO), National Parks/Biosphere reserves, basic Physics principles.
Trend: High integration with current affairs. Expect questions on AI basics, green energy, and recent ecological summits.

🎯 Difficulty & Question Types

Trend: "Statement-based" and "Match the following" types are replacing direct factual questions, pushing difficulty up.

🔄 Repetition & Topic Cycling

  • Exact Repeats: Rare post-2020 (< 2%).
  • Concept Repeats (High): e.g., They ask Article 14 one year, Article 19 the next, Article 21 the next. The *concept* of Fundamental Rights repeats.
  • 2-Year Cycle: Specific tribes and minor historical dynasties seem to rotate in/out every 2-3 years. If ignored in 2025/26, high probability for 2027.
