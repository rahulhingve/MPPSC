⚖️ Regulating Act, 1773

🔴 MUST REMEMBER 1773 = first step toward centralization; it did not make India directly governed by the Crown.
FeatureExam-ready meaning
Governor-General of BengalCreated the office; Warren Hastings was first.
Council of FourFour-member executive council assisted the Governor-General.
CentralizationBombay and Madras became subordinate in specified matters to the Bengal-centered system.
Supreme CourtCalcutta/Fort William; functioning from 1774; four judges.
Company servantsPrivate trade and acceptance of gifts/bribes were prohibited as emphasized in lecture.
Court of DirectorsReporting/accountability to the British government increased.
